We collect personal data (username, corporate email) and business data (inventory records, transaction history) necessary for the System's operation. Data is collected only when voluntarily provided by users.

All data is stored on secure servers with encryption (SSL/TLS) for data in transit and at rest. Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel only, and regular security audits are conducted.

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the System's functionality, comply with legal obligations, and prevent fraudulent activities. We will not share your data with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting our support team. You may also request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
